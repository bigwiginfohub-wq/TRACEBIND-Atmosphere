\# TRACEBIND Standardization Terminology



\*   \*\*Point ($p$):\*\* The fundamental spatial unit of measurement containing coordinate and scalar data.

\*   \*\*Region of Interest (ROI):\*\* The defined, bounded physical window extracted from a native dataset for processing.

\*   \*\*State Matrix ($I$):\*\* The 2D raster array representing the spatial field at a single slice of time.

\*   \*\*Reference Distribution (Null Model):\*\* The stochastic baseline generated by shuffling values exclusively within a localized geometric neighborhood ($k$), preserving sampling geometry while destroying spatial predictability.

\*   \*\*Localized Predictability Ratio ($R$):\*\* The core information-theoretic measurement quantifying the degree to which an observed spatial field is more or less predictable than the reference distribution baseline.

\*   \*\*Predictability:\*\* The extent to which local observations are reconstructed more accurately than localized randomized references.

\*   \*\*Measurement:\*\* A numerical quantity produced directly by the TRACEBIND core engine.

\*   \*\*Interpretation:\*\* A domain-dependent explanation generated completely outside the core mathematics.
